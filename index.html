About Our Brand

Redefining Everyday Luxury for Everyone in Kaduna.

At ChiNel Unisex Salon, we believe that self-care is not a privilege—it is an art form. Founded in the vibrant neighborhood of Narayi, near Ideal School, we bring runway-grade craftsmanship, clinical-level hygiene, and a warm, inclusive sanctuary together under one roof.

Whether you require a flawless lace closure install, traditional artistic braids, a razor-sharp executive beard fade, or sculpted nail art, our team crafts every look with uncompromising precision and individualized warmth.
